# SafeStage-4D — Open Source. Free Forever.

Earphones + cheap venue beacons that give you 4D sound that follows you, while never letting it go over a safe volume.

## How it works

Venue puts 2-3 Bluetooth beacons on the speaker stacks / stage. Fixed points.
Your earphones listen to them and work out how far you are, which way your head is turned, and how you're moving.

## 1. Concerts - 4D Sound

- Walk forward = fuller, punchier
- Walk back = clearer, less muddy
- Turn your head = sound moves with you
- Walk to the bar = softens. Walk back to the pit = opens up
- Walk between stages = hands over cleanly, like WiFi

Always capped at YOUR safe limit (85dB default, you can go lower).

**Protect Mode:** More cancellation the closer you get. Front row stays safe.
**Clarity Mode:** Less cancellation up close, more clarity boost further back. Back row sounds like front row.

User always wins. Venue suggests a safe limit, user can turn it DOWN further. Venue can NEVER turn it UP past safe.

## 2. Games

Same idea, virtual beacons. Free 4D spatial audio for any game dev.
No licence fees, no Dolby paywall.
Walk closer to a sound in-game, it gets louder but never blows your ears out. Turn your head, sound turns with you.

## 3. Deaf / Hard-of-Hearing

Boost the signal, not just the volume.
- Boost vocals, cut muddy bass
- Balance left/right if one ear is weaker
- Direct clean feed instead of fighting room echo
- Like a hearing aid + concert earphone in one, tuned for live music

## 4. Fire / Emergency

Music cuts instantly. Earphones give you clear voice directions:
> "Exit to your LEFT, 20m."

Updates as you move, because it knows where you are from the beacons.

For deaf users: vibration + visual + boosted alarm frequency.
Left buzz = go left, right buzz = go right.

Safe volume even in panic, so you're not deafened by the alarm itself while trying to escape.
